# JEEVAN SHIELD 🌍
### AI-Powered Air Pollution Source Identification and Mapping System

**Smarter Air. Healthier Tomorrow.**

JEEVAN SHIELD is an AI-powered environmental technology venture focused on identifying potential sources of air pollution in urban and industrial areas. The platform combines air-quality, weather, traffic, and industrial data to help pollution-control authorities investigate pollution hotspots and take targeted action.

**Institution:** Sri Sairam Engineering College, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India

## 📌 Problem Statement

Urban and industrial areas face difficulties in identifying the specific sources of air pollution, including vehicle emissions, industrial smoke, construction dust, and waste burning.

Although air-quality monitoring data is available, identifying the sources responsible for pollution remains challenging. This limits the ability of authorities to take targeted action, protect public health, and improve environmental management.

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), 99% of the global population breathes air that exceeds WHO air-quality guideline limits.

Source: [WHO — Air Pollution](https://www.who.int/health-topics/air-pollution)

## 💡 Our Solution

JEEVAN SHIELD proposes an AI-powered pollution-source identification and mapping system initially focused on Chennai.

The platform analyzes air-quality data alongside weather conditions, traffic information, and industrial data to identify potential pollution sources and visualize pollution hotspots.

It aims to help environmental authorities prioritize inspections, understand pollution patterns, and make informed decisions using location-specific insights.

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI-Based Pollution Source Identification:** Identifies potential sources contributing to pollution patterns.
- **Air Quality Monitoring:** Presents air-quality information and pollution trends.
- **Pollution Hotspot Mapping:** Visualizes pollution hotspots using map-based dashboards.
- **Weather-Based Analysis:** Uses weather information to support pollution analysis.
- **Location-Based Alerts:** Provides alerts associated with identified pollution hotspots.
- **Data Visualization:** Displays environmental information through an interactive dashboard.
- **Verified Data Records:** Proposes blockchain-based records to improve traceability.
- **Actionable Reports:** Helps authorities investigate pollution hotspots and prioritize inspections.

## ⚙️ Technology Stack

- **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning:** Pollution pattern analysis and potential source identification.
- **IoT and Air-Quality Sensors:** Environmental data collection and planned real-time monitoring.
- **GIS Mapping:** Geographical visualization of pollution hotspots.
- **Data Analytics:** Analysis of air-quality, weather, traffic, and industrial information.
- **Blockchain:** Proposed verification and traceability of pollution records.
- **Cloud Computing:** Data storage, processing, and dashboard support.
- **Web Dashboard:** Visualization of pollution data, maps, alerts, and records.

## 🔄 Working Principle

1. **Data Collection:** Gather available air-quality, weather, traffic, and industrial information.
2. **Data Processing:** Organize and process the collected environmental data.
3. **AI Analysis:** Analyze pollution patterns to identify potential contributing sources.
4. **Geographical Mapping:** Display pollution hotspots and related insights on a Chennai map.
5. **Alerts and Reports:** Present location-based alerts and reports to support investigation.
6. **Targeted Action:** Help pollution-control authorities prioritize inspections and plan appropriate interventions.
